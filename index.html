Julio Sampietro Christ

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Instituto de Matemáticas of UNAM in Cuernavaca, as part of the Simons Geometry Program. I did my PhD at Université Paris-Saclay under the supervision of Claude Viterbo.

I work in symplectic topology, mostly on Floer theory: Lagrangian Floer (co)homology, its equivariant counterpart and how they interact with symplectic reduction. More broadly, I am interested in topology and geometry.
