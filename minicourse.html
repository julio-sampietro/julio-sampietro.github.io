Lagrangian Floer (co)homology

A mini-course, fall 2026.

Wednesdays at 15:30
Instituto de Matemáticas, UNAM, Unidad Cuernavaca

These lectures are an introduction to the basics of Lagrangian Floer theory, for people who want to understand how it works and how to use it. Notes for each lecture are posted below as they become available.

Lectures

  1. Definitions and motivation
    Symplectic manifolds and cotangent bundles; isotropic, coisotropic and Lagrangian submanifolds; Hamiltonian isotopies; the Arnol’d conjecture and Floer’s theorem.
    [notes]
  2. Morse theory recap
    Morse functions and the Morse lemma; gradient flow, stable and unstable manifolds; the Morse complex; transversality and Morse–Smale pairs; compactness, broken trajectories and ∂² = 0; the example of the circle.
    [notes]
  3. Finite-dimensional transversality
    Transverse maps and fibered products; sections of vector bundles and generic transversality (Thom, Sard); parametrised families of sections; a proof that Morse functions are open and dense.
    [notes]
